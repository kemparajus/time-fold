Assuming warmer and more variable rainfall, hypothesis is that heat and water stress could increase vegetation maintenance needs and surface reflection, but this is not a prediction.

CONFIDENCE AND LIMITS
- Moderate confidence for terminal expansion and greening/infill, which are visually verified; low confidence for exact counts and area_share, which are rough bounding-box estimates prone to fragmentation and false positives.
